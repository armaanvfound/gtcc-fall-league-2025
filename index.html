Wickets in hand are worth 35 runs

Nearly a third of all innings in this league ended all out. Simply batting out your 15 overs — regardless of how fast you score — is the single largest lever on the scoreboard.

    Qualification is a group of six

    The 30 teams played five round-robin matches inside five groups of six. The top three in each group went through — fifteen teams, exactly half the field. Three wins from five put you on the bubble, and the bubble was broken by head-to-head and net run rate.
